GOOD TO KNOW

Questions & answers.

How do I sign in?

Continue with Google, or set up a profile with just a name and a password. Each profile has its own chats and notebooks, so people who share a computer stay separate.

What if I forget my password?

When you make a profile, you get a recovery code. Save it somewhere safe. On the sign-in screen, choose "Forgot your password?" and enter the code to set a new password. You can make a new code any time from your Account page.

Where is my data stored?

Your conversations, notebooks, plans, and quiz results are stored in your browser. Messages you send go to the AI model to generate an answer. Clearing browser data, or using another device or browser, means you won't see your saved history.

What can I upload?

Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF), PDFs, and plain-text, Markdown, or CSV files: up to 1 MB each, with up to 4 files and 2 MB per message.

Will it just do my homework for me?

No. EduCator is built to never hand over answers to homework or tests. It explains ideas, gives hints step by step, and checks your thinking, so you can do the work yourself and actually understand it.

What's the difference between a chat and a notebook?

Chats are for quick questions. Notebooks are for subjects you want to study over time: a diagnostic, a learning plan that adapts to you, guided lessons, quizzes, review, and progress tracking. Their sessions stay private to that notebook.

Is the AI always right?

EduCator is usually accurate but can make mistakes. Double-check important facts, especially for exams, and ask follow-up questions when something doesn't seem right.
