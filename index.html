GOOD TO KNOW

Questions & answers.

Do I need an account?

No. EduCator works without signing up. Your chats and notebooks are saved in your browser on this device.

Where is my data stored?

Your conversations, notebooks, plans, and quiz results are stored in your browser. Messages you send go to the AI model to generate an answer. Clearing browser data, or using another device or browser, means you won't see your saved history.

What can I upload?

Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF), PDFs, and plain-text, Markdown, or CSV files: up to 1 MB each, with up to 4 files and 2 MB per message.

Will it just do my homework for me?

EduCator is designed to help you learn. It explains ideas, gives hints, and checks your thinking, so you understand the material and not just the answer.

What's the difference between a chat and a notebook?

Chats are for quick questions. Notebooks are for subjects you want to study over time, with a learning plan, guided lessons, quizzes, and progress tracking. Their sessions stay private to that notebook.

Is the AI always right?

EduCator is usually accurate but can make mistakes. Double-check important facts, especially for exams, and ask follow-up questions when something doesn't seem right.
