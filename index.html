Why does ice float on water?
Great question! It's all about density.

When water freezes, its molecules lock into a hexagonal lattice held by hydrogen bonds, which leaves more empty space than liquid water.

ρ = m / V  →  ρice ≈ 0.92 g/cm³ < ρwater ≈ 1.00 g/cm³

Less dense things float, so ice sits on top. 🧊

Questions

Good to know.

Do I need an account?

No. EduCator works without signing up. Your chats and notebooks are saved in your browser on this device.

Where is my data stored?

Your conversations, notebooks, plans, and quiz results are stored locally in your browser. Messages you send are passed to the AI model to generate an answer. Clearing your browser data, or switching to another device or browser, means you won't see your saved history.

What can I upload?

Images (JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF), PDFs, and plain-text, Markdown, or CSV files. Each file can be up to 1 MB, with up to 4 files and 2 MB per message.

Will it just do my homework for me?

EduCator is designed to help you learn. It explains ideas, gives hints, and checks your thinking, so you understand the material and not just the answer.

What's the difference between a chat and a notebook?

Chats are great for quick questions. Notebooks are for subjects you want to study over time: they include a personalised learning plan, guided lessons, quizzes, and progress tracking, and their sessions stay private to that notebook.

Is the AI always right?

EduCator is usually accurate but can make mistakes. Double-check important facts, especially for exams, and ask follow-up questions whenever something doesn't seem right.
